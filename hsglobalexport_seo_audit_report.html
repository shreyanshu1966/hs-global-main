Critical issues

must fix
  • No robots.txt file detected
    Search engines have no crawl instructions. Create one at /robots.txt and reference your sitemap.
  • No XML sitemap found
    No sitemap.xml at the domain root. Google/Bing can't efficiently discover all product pages. Generate and submit to Search Console.
  • Zero structured data / JSON-LD schema
    No Product, Organization, BreadcrumbList, or FAQ schema on any page. Rich results (star ratings, prices) are completely blocked.
  • Product pages lack Product schema
    Individual pages like /product/beige-stone-bathtub-… have prices and availability but no schema — Google can't show price in SERPs.
  • Footer policy links resolve to # anchors
    User Agreement, Privacy, Shipping Policy, Cookie Preferences, and Site Map all link to "#" — broken for users and signals low trust to Google.

Warnings

should fix
  • Homepage title is 100+ characters (over limit)
    "HS Global Export : Premium Granite & Marble Solutions - Handcrafted Products Manufacturers with Free Delivery | USA | UK" — Google truncates at ~60 chars. Rewrite to ~55 chars with primary keyword first.
  • Meta description has a typo ("marble furniturers")
    Homepage and OG description contain "Best marble furniturers" — a misspelling that appears on every shared link.
  • Product OG title truncated at "..." on all product pages
    og:title is cut to "Beige Stone Bathtub Freestanding Marble Tub Rustic Carved..." — social previews will look incomplete. Keep og:title under 60 chars.
  • Blog section has only 1 article
    The /blog page shows a single post (June 2026). Thin content signals low authority. Publish at least 1–2 posts per month consistently.
  • No customer reviews / social proof on product pages
    All product pages show "No reviews yet". Zero reviews = no review schema, no star ratings, and lower conversion. Encourage early buyers to review.
  • Products page shows "0 items" in filter UI
    /products renders "0 items" and "48 of 0" in JavaScript — suggests client-side rendering without SSR fallback. Googlebot may not see product count correctly.
  • Canonical URL mismatch: http vs https/www
    Homepage fetched via hsglobalexport.com (no www) redirected to www.hsglobalexport.com, but canonical tag is set to https://www.hsglobalexport.com — check this is consistent in all headers.
  • About page claims "50 years" heritage but company founded 2024
    The about page says "nearly 50 years" and a "1985" chapter but trade databases show 2024 founding. Conflicting signals reduce E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust).

Opportunities

nice to have
  • Add BreadcrumbList schema to product and category pages
    Breadcrumbs appear in the UI (Home / furniture / product name) but no JSON-LD schema backs them. Adding it enables breadcrumb rich results in Google.
  • Target long-tail keywords per product category
    High-value terms like "marble coffee table for sale USA", "freestanding stone bathtub buy online", or "custom granite furniture UK" are not in titles or H1s.
  • Add FAQ schema to product pages
    Product pages already have an FAQ accordion (shipping, customs, returns). Wrap these in FAQPage JSON-LD to potentially claim FAQ rich results.
  • Expand internal linking from blog to products
    The one blog article doesn't appear to link to specific product pages. Every post should link to 2–4 relevant products to distribute authority.

Passing

good work
  • Meta robots set to "index, follow" on all pages
  • Open Graph tags (og:title, og:description, og:image, og:url) present on every page
  • Twitter Card tags (summary_large_image) present on all pages
  • Canonical URLs set and self-referencing correctly on each page
  • Viewport meta tag present (mobile-ready)
  • Images served via Cloudinary with f_auto, q_auto (format + quality optimization) and Next.js image optimization
  • Unique H1 on each page; logical heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3) present
